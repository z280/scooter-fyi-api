Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mile High Technology Consulting, LLC, doing business as "scooter.fyi" ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and retains information in connection with scooter.fyi, including denver.scooter.fyi (the "Service").

The retention figures below are also published in machine-readable form at GET /api/v1/meta/privacy; that live endpoint is the enforced source of truth — if this document and the API response ever disagree, the API reflects what the code actually does.

You don't need an account to use the map. The public map, device data, and compliance metrics are available anonymously. We collect personal information only when you choose to create an account, submit a report, or subscribe.

2. How we use information

  • Operate the Service (sign-in, sessions, the cost ticker, ride history).
  • Build and publish the public compliance dataset — device reports and discount reports feed aggregate, anonymized public statistics (the regional reports summary and the monthly CSV export). Aggregate publications never include your identity, IP address, or email.
  • Detect and prevent abuse (rate limiting, fraudulent reports).
  • Respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access/export: your ride history is available via GET /api/v1/rides/export in your account.
  • Delete rides: DELETE /api/v1/rides/:id or DELETE /api/v1/rides at any time — immediate and irreversible.
  • Delete your account: email us (below); we don't yet have self-serve account deletion.
  • Opt out of usage analytics: the "Allow private analytics" switch in the About Scooter.fyi panel turns off all usage-event collection for that browser. The choice is stored on your device and works whether or not you're signed in. Browsers sending Global Privacy Control are opted out automatically.
  • We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of in that sense.

If you're in a jurisdiction with statutory data-subject rights (e.g. CCPA/CPRA, GDPR), contact us and we'll do our best to honor equivalent requests even where not strictly required, given the size of this project.

6. Security

Session tokens and magic-link tokens are stored hashed, never in plaintext. All traffic is served over HTTPS. Receipt images and model-report photos live in a private storage bucket that is never publicly readable. No system is perfectly secure; if you discover a vulnerability, please report it to us directly before disclosing it publicly.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
