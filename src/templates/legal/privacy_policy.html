2. How we use information

  • Operate the Service (sign-in, sessions, the cost ticker, ride history).
  • Build and publish the public compliance dataset — device reports and discount reports feed aggregate, anonymized public statistics (the regional reports summary and the monthly CSV export). Aggregate publications never include your identity, IP address, or email.
  • Detect and prevent abuse (rate limiting, fraudulent reports).
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Reading receipt images: not yet. We do not currently read receipt images automatically — you type in the minutes and the costs yourself. If we start, we will update this policy first. That reading may happen on your device or on our servers via OpenRouter, and it will apply only to an image you chose to upload.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access/export: rides you logged on vehicles not in the operator’s feed can be exported via GET /api/v1/rides/export; Ride Mode rides can be listed via GET /api/v1/tracked-rides (there is no export for these yet).
  • Delete rides: DELETE /api/v1/tracked-rides/:id or DELETE /api/v1/tracked-rides for Ride Mode rides, and DELETE /api/v1/rides/:id or DELETE /api/v1/rides for those other rides, at any time — immediate and irreversible.
  • Hide your username and standing: Account → Community has the two visibility switches (both on by default).
  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any text (UNSTOP to opt back in). This also stops texts from our other services that share the same sending number.
  • Delete your account: email us (below); we don't yet have self-serve account deletion.
  • Opt out of usage analytics: the "Allow private analytics" switch in the About Scooter.fyi panel turns off all usage-event collection for that browser. The choice is stored on your device and works whether or not you're signed in. Browsers sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track are opted out automatically.
  • We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of in that sense.

If you're in a jurisdiction with statutory data-subject rights (e.g. CCPA/CPRA, GDPR), contact us and we'll do our best to honor equivalent requests even where not strictly required, given the size of this project.

6. Security

Session tokens, sign-in links and sign-in codes are stored hashed, never in plaintext. Saved places are encrypted with a key the database does not hold, so the row that holds your Home address is ciphertext in our database, in every backup of it, and in any copy either one ends up in (apart from the older plain-text copies described in §1). All traffic is served over HTTPS. Receipt images, model-report photos and ride screenshots live in a private storage bucket that is never publicly readable. (Scooter photos are different: they are public content, see §1.) No system is perfectly secure; if you discover a vulnerability, please report it to us directly before disclosing it publicly.

Two limits worth stating plainly, because the alternative is letting the word “encrypted” do more work than it should. We hold the decryption key, so a compromise of our running service could read your saved places, and so could we — encryption at rest protects the data where it sits, not from us. And it covers saved places only: your email address, phone number and ride history are stored as ordinary columns.
